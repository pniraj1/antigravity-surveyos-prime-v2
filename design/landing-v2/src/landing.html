02 — Collection

Stop running behind
the papers.

Half a survey is waiting on somebody else. The insured has the licence, the workshop has the estimate, the insurer wants the whole file. SurveyOS Sync lets them send a document to a Telegram bot, and it lands in the claim ready to attach.

Every pending document across every open claim, on one screen. Chase all of them in one tap, or stop chasing.

Why this exists

I watched my father
type until midnight.

He has been a motor surveyor for most of my life. I have watched him drive two hundred kilometres in a day, come home, eat, and then start the report at ten at night. The report lived on one desktop computer. If he needed it at a garage the next morning, it may as well not have existed.

I am a surveyor too. So is my brother. Between the three of us we have typed the same chassis number into the same form more times than I can count.

None of that is surveying. Surveying is looking at a damaged vehicle, deciding what it will honestly cost to put right, and putting your licence to that number. Everything else is admin that grew around the job.

Motor SurveyOS exists to take the admin away. Documents are read so nobody retypes them. The claim file is on your phone, not on a desktop at home. What is left is the part that needs a surveyor — and that part stays yours. The software extracts, reconciles and flags. It does not decide. Your name and your licence are on the report, so the judgement has to be too.
