SurveyOS Scan: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SurveyOS Scan ("the app") helps motor surveyors scan claim documents and photos on their phone and organise them into claim folders.

What the app does with your data

  • Your claims, scans and photos stay on your phone. The app has no server and the developer does not receive, collect or see your documents, photos or claim details.
  • Files leave your phone only when you ask. That means tapping Share or Save to folder, or turning on Google Drive backup (below).
  • Google Drive backup (optional, off by default). If you turn it on in Settings and sign in with Google, the app copies claim text (details, notes, contacts, document status and request history) and, in "Full" mode, the claim's PDFs and photos into a SurveyOS folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see only files it created itself, never the rest of your Drive. The developer has no access to your Drive and receives nothing. Motor SurveyOS Prime, signed in with the same Google account, can read and update these files. Turning backup off signs the app out of Drive; files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • Google user data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Drive data is used only to back up and sync your claims, is never sold, never used for ads, and never read by people.
  • Photo stamp and location. If you allow location, the app reads the phone's position when you take a photo and writes the date, time, address and GPS coordinates on the photo and into its image details. When the phone is online, the coordinates are sent to Android's address lookup (Geocoder, provided by Google Play services) to get the street address. The app itself keeps the location only on your phone, with the photo. You can turn the stamp off in Settings, or refuse location.
  • Safe copy in Gallery. By default the app keeps a copy of every page you add (photos, scans, imports and shared files) in your phone's shared Pictures/SurveyOS Scan folder, so they survive if the app is deleted. Each copy keeps the photo's date and, if the stamp had one, its GPS location in the image details. These copies stay on your phone; the app never uploads them, but other apps with access to your photos can read them. If you use a backup app such as Google Photos, it may back them up under its own settings. You can turn this off in Settings; existing copies stay until you delete them in your Gallery.
  • Text backup. The app keeps each claim's details, notes, contacts and request history as files in your phone's shared Documents/SurveyOS Scan folder, so you can restore your claims after reinstalling or on a new phone (Settings > Restore from phone backup). These files stay on your phone; the app never uploads them, but other apps with storage access, and backup apps, may read them.
  • Contacts. For each claim you can type the insured's and garage's name, phone and email, or pick one from your phone's contacts. The app has no contacts permission: when you pick, Android hands it only the one name and number you chose. "Save to phone" opens your Contacts app with the details filled in, and you tap Save there. Claim contacts are stored on your phone, in the text backup, in claim folders you choose to share and, if you turned it on, in your Google Drive backup.
  • Asking for documents. When you tap WhatsApp or Email, the app opens that app with a message ready; you tap Send there. The app itself sends nothing.
  • Document scanning. Scanning uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs on your phone through Google Play services. Your images are not sent anywhere by it. Google may collect anonymous usage and diagnostic information from Play services, under Google's privacy policy.

Ads and purchases

  • The app shows a banner ad on the Home and Settings screens, served by Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalised. The AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google, as the ad provider: your device's advertising ID, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device information (such as model and Android version), how you interact with ads, and crash and diagnostic information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play; the developer does not see your payment details.

Security

Data sent between the app and Google (ads, Play, Drive) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete a claim in the app to remove its files from the app; its safe copies in Pictures/SurveyOS Scan stay until you delete them in your Gallery.
  • Files you shared or saved to a folder are copies you control; delete them where you saved them.
  • Drive backup files are in your Google Drive under SurveyOS/Phone Scans; delete them there, or from Motor SurveyOS Prime's cleanup screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your phone, except the safe copies in Pictures/SurveyOS Scan and the text backups in Documents/SurveyOS Scan, which you can delete with your Gallery or Files app.

Children

The app is for working professionals and is not directed at children.
